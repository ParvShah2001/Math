1. Foundational Tables (1 to 10) Shortcuts

  • 9's Finger/Digit Rule: Digits always add to 9 ($9 \times 4 = 36 \rightarrow 3+6=9$). The tens digit is $(n - 1)$.
  • 6's Even Rule: $6 \times \text{even digit}$ ends in that same digit ($6 \times 4 = 24$, $6 \times 8 = 48$).
  • 8's Double-Double-Double: $8 \times n = ((n \times 2) \times 2) \times 2$.
  • 7's Rhyme Anchor: $7 \times 8 = 56 \rightarrow$ consecutive digits 5, 6, 7, 8 ($56 = 7 \times 8$).

2. High Tables (11 to 25) Mental Splitting

Break into easy tens and units: $19 \times 7 = (20 \times 7) - 7 = 140 - 7 = 133$.
$14 \times 8 = (10 \times 8) + (4 \times 8) = 80 + 32 = 112$.

3. The "Ending in 5" Square Rule

For any number ending in 5 ($n5$): Multiply $n \times (n + 1)$, then append $25$.
$25^2 = 2 \times 3 = 6 \rightarrow 625$. $35^2 = 3 \times 4 = 12 \rightarrow 1225$.

4. Cube Unit-Digit Determinant

Cubes of $0, 1, 4, 5, 6, 9$ end with the same unit digit. Numbers ending in $2 \leftrightarrow 8$ and $3 \leftrightarrow 7$ swap with their complements to 10!
