1. The 9's Table Rule

For 9 × n: Tens digit is (n - 1). Units digit adds up to 9 with the tens digit. Example: 9 × 7 = 63 (6 and 3 add to 9).

2. Squaring Numbers Ending in 5

For any number ending in 5 (e.g. n5): multiply n × (n + 1), then write 25 at the end.
Example: 25² = (2 × 3) & 25 = 625. 35² = (3 × 4) & 25 = 1225.

3. Cubes Last Digit Invariant

Cubes of 0, 1, 4, 5, 6, 9 always end in the exact same last digit!
2 and 8 swap (2³=8, 8³=...2). 3 and 7 swap (3³=27, 7³=...3).

4. Tables 11 to 25 Mental Splitting

Split into 10s and units:
19 × 7 = (20 × 7) - 7 = 140 - 7 = 133.
14 × 8 = (10 × 8) + (4 × 8) = 80 + 32 = 112.
